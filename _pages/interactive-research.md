## A little extra: "Armchair Cognitive Science"
I call this task "armchair cognitive science". Armchair, as it echoes armchair philosophy, where we think about our intuitions and introspect on our experiences. Cognitive science, as the question follows the instructional format of a cognitive task.

The task is simple: there will be one question (with some variations), after which you can indicate your response freely.

The origins of this task derive from my own intuitions (from my armchair). Since I do not wish to affect your experience in response to the question in any way, I am not providing any more details on the background of this task. There is no theory, no hypotheses. Rest assured, there is no right or wrong answer either.

**Please Note! By continuing to the external link, you agree that your answer may be used (anonymously) for future research purposes should the opportunity arise.**

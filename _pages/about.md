I call myself an emerging cognitive scientist. On the one hand, I am changing the direction of my career from data management and analysis to empirical sciences. On the other hand, I am not quite "there" yet (i.e., I am currently not affiliated with an academic institution).

Why mental imagery? When I was a first-year Psychology undergraduate, one of our lecturers started his class by an exercise, whereby he asked the audience to imagine something. After a short pause, he went on and said that _some of you might not be able to imagine what I said_ (paraphrasing). This was the first time I heard about those people who do not have visual mental imagery (i.e., aphantasia). I was flabbergasted. Is it possible that this intuitive and so evident inner experience of mine (my mental images) do not exist for others? How could that be? I call this "my aphantasia paradox"; I cannot imagine those who cannot imagine. This puzzlement started my scientific journey into the depths and the abyss of mental imagery.

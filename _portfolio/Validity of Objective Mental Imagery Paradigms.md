Abstract
======
People with aphantasia report a lack of voluntary visual mental imagery based on a subjective measurement, while there are indications that their involuntary mental imagery capabilities may be spared. Furthermore, performance on objective tasks of visual mental imagery is comparable between people with and without aphantasia. One suggestion for such a discrepancy between subjective and objective measurements is that people with aphantasia utilise an alternative strategy to visual mental imagery. This interpretation raises the question whether such objective tasks are valid measurement tools of visual mental imagery. To explore this idea, different instructions can be employed in these tasks; an approach that is reminiscent of the mental imagery debate. Therefore, the goal of this research programme is to replicate studies of objective tasks of visual mental imagery by comparing the effects of different instructions between people with and without aphantasia.
